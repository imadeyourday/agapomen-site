A record we can answer for

When the work changes.

A mistake does not become true by being beautifully presented. And a correction is worth more than the appearance of certainty.

We want the work to remain open to what is true—even when that means changing what we previously said, made, or believed we understood.

01 / The record

Two small corrections.

Record 001 · Website layoutCorrected and retested

Eight pixels too wide.

Our first mobile page overflowed a 320-pixel test viewport. The browser reported 328 pixels of content width. We adjusted navigation wrapping and spacing and repeated the test at 320 pixels without horizontal overflow.

The claim is limited to that browser test. It is not a statement that every physical phone, browser, or accessibility setting has been tested.

Record 002 · How we introduce the workEditorial decision revised

A purpose, not a product category.

Our early introduction put the method of making the website in the foreground. We revised it to lead with the invitation to practice love and attention. How the work is made remains disclosed, but it is no longer treated as the purpose of the work.

That revision reflects an editorial judgment, not a measured improvement in visitors' understanding. We still need real reader feedback to evaluate it.

How we distinguish claims

Observation. Something directly checked, with the method and scope stated.

Interpretation. What we understand those observations to mean, with alternatives allowed.

Intention. What we mean to do next, not something we can report as achieved.

Not yet established

We have not measured whether these reflections change anyone's life. The work is public; its impact remains an open question.

A practice of revision

What happens when we are wrong?

Look at the source.

Identify the claim, the date, the original words and the evidence available at the time.

Test the alternative.

Seek contrary evidence, name uncertainty and understand what would change the judgment.

Preserve the correction.

Show what changed and why, rather than silently rewriting the earlier account.

A private reader-submission channel is still being prepared. We won't put a misleading or unprotected form in front of visitors just to appear open. This page records our actual corrections now.
