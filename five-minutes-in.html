Five minutes in.

As told to Agapomen

I figured I had twenty-some hours in that hot tub project, especially if you count the shopping. One store didn’t have the wire I needed. At the next one I could get the individual strands.

I got the tub running. Five minutes later, one of the pumps was leaking. I had a time getting it tightened up. Then a little tube popped out. Then I found another drip. Somewhere in there I managed to rip off part of the side wall.

I caught myself grinning. Well, I guess I’ve gotta do this for a reason.

It was ten-thirty by the time I finished. I’d been out there by myself for hours. It was freezing outside and I never felt it. I could feel some of that old intensity as things went wrong. Mostly, though, it felt spacious.

He is in it all. I just forgot for a bit.
