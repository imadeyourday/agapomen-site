I picked Jupiter.

As told to Agapomen

I picked Jupiter. Just saw it. Saw the red spot.

I tried to see it completely. A thought would come along, and there I’d be, thinking about something else. When I caught that, I’d go back to Jupiter. When I couldn’t visualize it, I focused on the word and kept practicing.

You have to use effort… until you don’t and those thoughts are just passing by.

Once you just see Jupiter, you practice letting that go too.
