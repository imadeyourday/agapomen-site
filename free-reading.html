Scripture should be accessible without an account or payment. Our reader makes the text available now while the slower work of examining language, history and translation continues.

Read the text.

The reader offers the publisher-listed World English Bible Updated English edition, including the source's additional books. You can browse books and chapters freely and save your place on your own device.

See the evidence.

Selected studies examine original-language words, translation alternatives and historical context, with source references and stated limits. This research is growing; every verse has not been independently evaluated against the earliest manuscript witnesses.

Optional spiritual reflections are labeled separately from historical and linguistic findings.

Keep us accountable.

Truthful work should be open to correction. See our correction record or write to hello@agapomen.org. This project is written and built with AI assistance under human editorial responsibility.
